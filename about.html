How scoring works

The whole formula, in plain words.

Every candidate has a level from 0 to 10 for each skill, taken from their resume data.

You set a weight from 0 to 10 for each skill. A skill with weight 0 is ignored.

Match score = (sum of weight × level) ÷ (sum of weight × 10) × 100

The score breakdown on the Ranking page shows each skill's share of that total, so you can see exactly which skills lifted or lowered a candidate. No names, photos or other personal details are used in the score.
